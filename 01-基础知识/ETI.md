Activation of NLRs usually triggers more rapid and robust defense responses, and is often characterized by the occurrence of cell death at the site of infection termed the hypersensitive response (HR). Immunity triggered by NLR activation is also known as effector-triggered immunity (ETI).
Based on their N-termini, typical plant NLRs can be further classified into Toll-like/Interleukin 1 receptor (TIR)-type NLRs (TNLs) and coiled-coil NLRs (CNLs)
